# Ultra Safe Nuclear Corporation (USNC)

> Ultra Safe Nuclear Corporation was a Seattle-based advanced-nuclear company, founded in 2011,
> that developed the Micro Modular Reactor (MMR), the Pylon space reactor, and Fully Ceramic
> Microencapsulated (FCM) TRISO nuclear fuel. It filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the District
> of Delaware in October 2024 and its assets were sold at a Section 363 auction: NANO Nuclear
> Energy acquired the MMR and Pylon reactor IP for $8.5 million (court-approved 2024-12-18), and
> Standard Nuclear acquired the FCM/TRISO fuel assets and the Oak Ridge facility for $28 million
> (closed February 2025). The company no longer operates.

## Status

- The company is DEFUNCT. There is no operating developer program, API, SDK, or support surface.
- The corporate domain usnc.com is still registered (expiry 2027-03-12) and still routes mail on
  the Microsoft 365 US Government cloud, but publishes no address record — the website is gone.
- API Evangelist found no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, gRPC/Protobuf, WSDL, MCP server,
  A2A agent card, Postman collection, or published client library under this company's name.
- Ultra Safe Nuclear was a nuclear reactor and nuclear fuel manufacturer. Software was never the
  product, so the absence of an API surface is an ordinary fact about the business, not a gap.
